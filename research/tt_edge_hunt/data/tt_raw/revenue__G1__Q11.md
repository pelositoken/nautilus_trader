### How this was built
- Metric: revenue (fees retained after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses), read as a daily flow summed per project per calendar day.
- Window: December 1, 2024 through February 28, 2025, as requested.
- Entities: Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, NEAR Protocol and Uniswap, each read whole-project (all products combined).
- Each cell is the project's revenue on that day. Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain and NEAR Protocol have a value every day in the window; Uniswap has no value returned on any day, shown as "-".
- One project's daily figures are per day and are not aggregated into a total. No total over the window is given, and the series is not summed or averaged.
