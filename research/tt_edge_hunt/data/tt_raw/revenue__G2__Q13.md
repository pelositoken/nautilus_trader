### How this was built
- Cohort: Ethena, Tron, Sui, Chainlink and Avalanche, each matched by its exact registry name (all public, non-archived projects).
- Window: 2025-06-01 to 2025-08-31 inclusive, one row per calendar day (92 days).
- Metric: daily revenue in USD — the portion of fees each project retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses. The figure shown for each project each day is that project's total revenue for the day (its deployment-level rows summed per day).
- Every one of the 92 days had an observation for all five projects, so no day needed a "-" placeholder.
- Each project's revenue rows span well before and after this window (Ethena from November 20, 2023; Tron from June 25, 2018; Sui from May 3, 2023; Chainlink from June 1, 2023; Avalanche from January 6, 2021), so no part of the window is a late start.
