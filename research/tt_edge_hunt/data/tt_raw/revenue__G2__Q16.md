### How this was built
- Window: March 1, 2026 through May 31, 2026 (92 calendar days), the exact range requested; every calendar day is present, whether or not a value was observed.
- Revenue is the project's own revenue (fee revenue retained after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses), summed to one value per project per day at the whole-project grain.
