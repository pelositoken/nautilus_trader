### How this was built
- Cohort: the five projects named in the question, resolved in the registry to Curve, Aptos, Jupiter, Celestia and Render Network. Each is shown as a column.
- Metric: revenue — the portion of fees a project retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses — reported in USD.
- Window: 2022-09-01 to 2022-11-30 (91 calendar days), one row per day, no cross-day aggregation. Each cell is that project's revenue for that single day.
- Source: project-level daily revenue totals.
- Coverage: Curve returned a value on all 91 days. Aptos returned values from 2022-10-18 onward (its first observed day in the window); earlier days are not observed. Jupiter, Celestia and Render Network returned no revenue observations at all in this window, so their columns are empty (the "no data" case).
- A blank cell means no revenue was observed for that project on that day, not zero.
