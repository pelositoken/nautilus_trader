### How this was built
- The measure is revenue (the portion of fees a project retains after supply-side payouts), read per project per calendar day and summed across each day's rows over the window 2024-03-01 to 2024-05-31.
- Entities: Polygon, PancakeSwap, OP Mainnet (listed as "OP Mainnet"), Raydium and Ether.fi. Each was matched by exact registry name.
- Every one of the 92 calendar days in the window is shown, one row per day, with the five projects as columns.
- Ether.fi has no revenue observation on any day in this window; those cells are shown as "—" (no value observed), not as zero.
- Values are the projects' own project-level revenue, not a chain's or product's subset.
