### How this was built
- Daily revenue (USD) for the five named projects, one row per calendar day from 2024-12-01 to 2025-02-28; each project's revenue read from its own project-level rows and summed to the day.
- Values are the portion of fees each project retains, before cost of revenue and operating expenses. A day with no value is shown as "-" (no observation returned for that project and day).
- Polygon, PancakeSwap, OP Mainnet, Raydium and Ether.fi were resolved to their canonical project records (all five PUBLIC, not archived); the whole 90-day window was returned for each.
- A whole-project row is used for each project, so each project's figure is its total across its products.
