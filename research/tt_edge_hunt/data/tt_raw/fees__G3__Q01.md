### How this was built
- Fees are gross user-paid fees summed per calendar day, one row per day, 2022-06-01 through 2022-08-31, read from each entity's project-level fee rows.
- Aave is the lending project; Injective, Filecoin and Arbitrum One are read as their own chain projects.
- Ondo Finance's fees are not tracked, so it could not be included; the rest of the request is answered without it.
- Days with no observation for a project are left empty rather than shown as an observed zero.
- The window's last day in the data is 2022-08-31.
