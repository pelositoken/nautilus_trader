### How this was built
- One row per calendar day from September 1 to November 30, 2022 (91 days), with each project as its own column; values are that day's circulating supply (token count) read as a point-in-time balance, not aggregated over the window.
- The five projects are the named entities resolved by exact registry match: Ethena, Tron, Sui, Chainlink and Avalanche.
- Circulating supply is the number of tokens available for trading on the open market; it excludes locked, unvested, and project-treasury tokens. A dash marks a day with no observation for that project.
- Ethena and Sui have no circulating-supply observation on any day in this window; every day for them is shown as a dash. Tron, Chainlink and Avalanche have a value on essentially every day, with Chainlink stepping from 467,099,986 to 491,599,971 on September 20, 2022.
