### How this was built
- Each cell is one entity's **fees** on that calendar day, in USD: the total value users paid to use it that day. Fees are gross user spend, not the value the project retains.
- The five entities are read from their own project-level rows: Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain and NEAR Protocol (each a chain's own row) and Uniswap (a project).
- Window: March 1 to May 31, 2025 — 92 calendar days, shown one row per day with no period aggregation.
- All five entities have an observation on every one of the 92 days, so no day needed a "-" entry.
