### How this was built
- Cohort: the five named projects — Ethena, Tron, Sui, Chainlink and Avalanche — matched by their registry names.
- Metric: revenue, the portion of fees a project retains after supply-side payouts, reported in USD.
- Window: 2022-12-01 through 2023-02-28 (90 calendar days); each day is that day's revenue, no aggregation.
- Days with no observation are shown as `-`: Ethena, Sui and Chainlink returned no revenue rows for any day in this window, so every cell under those three columns is blank. Avalanche and Tron returned a value on all 90 days.
- The table is one row per day and one column per project, as requested; the underlying result is one row per (day, project).
