### How this was built
- Each project's daily circulating supply (a point-in-time token count) is read as its latest observation for that calendar day, with Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, NEAR Protocol and Uniswap resolved to their project entities.
- The window runs 2025-09-01 to 2025-11-30; every calendar day is included, and a day with no tracked observation shows no value.
- The figure is circulating supply — tokens available on the open market, excluding locked, unvested, and treasury-held tokens.
