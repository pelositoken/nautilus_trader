### How this was built
- Cohort: the five named projects — Curve, Aptos, Jupiter, Celestia, and Render Network — resolved to their canonical project identities and read as-is.
- Metric: circulating token supply, a point-in-time token count (not summed across days), read at one row per calendar day.
- Window: December 1, 2022 through February 28, 2023 — 90 calendar days, returned in full with no aggregation.
- Source: project-level daily observations, one value per project per day.
- Jupiter and Celestia returned no circulating-supply observation on any day in this window, so every one of their cells is "-".
- The full-grain rows are available as a CSV export on request.
