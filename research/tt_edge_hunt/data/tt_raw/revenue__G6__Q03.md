### How this was built
- Cohort: the five named projects — Sei Network, Lido Finance, Cosmos Hub, Pendle and Jito — each read at whole-project grain.
- Window: every calendar day from 2022-12-01 through 2023-02-28.
- Metric: revenue, the portion of fees a project retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses, shown in USD at daily grain.
- Source: project-level daily metrics; each date's figure is that day's revenue, with no period aggregation.
- A cell with no observation for that project and day is shown as "-".
