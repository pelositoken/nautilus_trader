### How this was built
- The table holds each project's onchain revenue — the portion of fees it retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses — summed for each calendar day of the requested range, one row per day and no aggregation across days.
- Window: June 1 – August 31, 2022, the range the question named.
- Values come from the project revenue series (the project/surface grain); Arbitrum One is read as its own chain project row.
- Coverage over the window: Aave, Injective and Filecoin each have an observation on all 92 days; Arbitrum One has a single observation, on August 31, 2022, so its other 91 days show no data and are left blank. A blank cell means no observation was recorded for that project on that day (not a zero).
