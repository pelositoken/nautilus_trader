### How this was built
- Figures are the daily project revenue for Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain, NEAR Protocol and Uniswap, one value per calendar day; a "-" marks a day with no observation for that project.
- Window: September 1, 2023 to November 30, 2023 (91 calendar days), every day included whether or not a value was observed.
- Revenue is the fee portion a project retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses, read from the project metrics surface.
- Coverage: Uniswap has no revenue observation on any day in this window (its revenue series begins December 27, 2025), so its column is "-" throughout. Ethereum, Solana, BNB Chain and NEAR Protocol have a value on every day of the window.
