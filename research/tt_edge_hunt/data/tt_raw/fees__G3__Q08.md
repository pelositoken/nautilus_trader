### How this was built
- Daily fees (the gross value users pay to use each project) for Aave, Injective, Filecoin and Arbitrum One, one row per calendar day and one column per project, covering March 1 – May 31, 2024 (92 calendar days); each day is a summed figure and days are not aggregated together.

- All four projects have an observation on every one of the 92 days, so no day is empty.
