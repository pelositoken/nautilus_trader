### How this was built
- Revenue is the portion of fees a project retains after supply-side payouts, before cost of revenue and operating expenses; each project's daily value is the sum of its revenue observations for that calendar day.
- The window is June 1, 2026 through August 31, 2026; it was fixed by the question, and every calendar day in it is present, with a day a project has no revenue observation left blank rather than shown as zero (Render Network has no revenue observations in this window, so its column is empty throughout).
- Projects were resolved by exact registry match: Curve, Aptos, Jupiter, Celestia, Render Network.
- Revenue is read from the project surface at project level (all of each project's products combined), one row per project per day.
