### How this was built
- Cohort: the five projects named — Ethena, Tron, Sui, Chainlink and Avalanche — each resolved by its canonical registry entry.
- Measure: circulating supply, the count of tokens currently available for trading on the open market, read as a point-in-time balance at its latest value on each day, per project, across the whole project (no chain or product split).
- Window: 2023-09-01 to 2023-11-30, one row per calendar day per project, with no aggregation across days.
- One part not reached: Ethena returned no observed circulating-supply value on any date in this window; those cells are shown as “-”.
