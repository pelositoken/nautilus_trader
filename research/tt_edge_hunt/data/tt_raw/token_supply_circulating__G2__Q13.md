### How this was built
- Cohort: the five named entities — Ethena and Chainlink as projects, and Tron, Sui and Avalanche as their own chain rows.
- Measure: circulating supply (token count) for each entity, read as a daily point-in-time level from its native deployments.

- Each entity returned a value on every one of the 92 days, so no day is blank.
